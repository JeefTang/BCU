Method & constants. Methane LHV 35.8 MJ/Nm³ = 9.94 kWh/Nm³. Biogas energy = flow × CH₄ fraction × 9.94. Electrical output = fuel energy × electrical efficiency. Recoverable heat = fuel energy × heat fraction. All results are indicative; guarantees are issued only against measured site data.

Why we publish the formulas. Most supplier calculators hide their assumptions. A biogas project is decided on gas quality and operating hours, not on a polished number.
