Tool 07

Biogas heat-pump air-conditioning (BHP) → cooling without the grid

Offices, laboratory and worker housing; exclude process cooling.
Engine-driven class: 0.95–1.15 depending on ambient and part load.
97% biomethane is the standard fuel; raw biogas needs derating and shorter service intervals.
Indicative RM 2,500/kW cooling, installation excluded.

Grid 0.35–0.50; diesel-generated 0.90–1.60 once fuel logistics and downtime are counted.

Engine service, oil and filters: 3–4% typical.

Method & constants. Methane LHV 35.8 MJ/Nm³ = 9.94 kWh/Nm³. Biogas energy = flow × CH₄ fraction × 9.94. Electrical output = fuel energy × electrical efficiency. Recoverable heat = fuel energy × heat fraction. All results are indicative; guarantees are issued only against measured site data.

Why we publish the formulas. Most supplier calculators hide their assumptions. A biogas project is decided on gas quality and operating hours, not on a polished number.
