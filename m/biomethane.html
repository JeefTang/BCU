Biomethane & bio-CNG

Biogas upgraded to boiler-grade gas

If the mill boiler runs on diesel, LPG or natural gas, the fastest payback is often not electricity — it is replacing that fuel with biomethane at ≥97% CH₄, produced on site.

Process

From 60% to 97% in five steps

DesulfuriseBiological plus iron oxide to protect media and compressor.

CompressTo the pressure required by the separation step.

DryRefrigerated dryer and coalescing filters.

Remove CO₂PSA, water scrubbing or membrane.

DeliverTo the burner, or 250 bar for tube-trailer bio-CNG.

Technology selection

PSA, water scrubbing or membrane

There is no universal best choice. It depends on gas volume, required purity, power cost and available space.

Gas quality we guarantee

  • CH₄ ≥ 97% (adjustable 96–99%)
  • CO₂ ≤ 2–3%
  • H₂S ≤ 5 ppm
  • Water dew point ≤ −40 °C at pressure
  • O₂ ≤ 0.5%

Burner retrofit is part of the scope. A biomethane burner needs a different gas train, orifice and flame detection from diesel or LPG — we coordinate that with the boiler supplier.
