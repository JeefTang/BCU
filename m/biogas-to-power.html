Biogas to power

From the pond to the busbar

A POME biogas plant is a chain, not a machine. Gas quality at the engine inlet decides whether you get 8,000 hours or 4,000 hours between overhauls.

Process chain

Six stages, one gas specification

CaptureCovered lagoon or CSTR; cover, condensate traps, collection headers.

BufferGas holder to absorb mill load swings.

DesulfuriseBiological scrubber plus iron-oxide polishing.

ConditionChilling, coalescing, blower to engine pressure.

GenerateGas engine and alternator, island or parallel. Brushless doubly-fed generator optional — see the generator comparison.

Recover heatJacket water ~90 °C and exhaust to hot water or steam.

Gas specification

What the engine will accept

These limits are the basis of our warranty discussion with the engine supplier. If your gas cannot meet them, the treatment scope changes — we would rather say so before the order.

← 左右滑动查看完整表格 / swipe to see all columns
Methane content≥ 50% (design 55–65%)
H₂S at engine inlet≤ 200 ppm, target ≤ 100
Water dew point≤ 10 °C above ambient
Gas temperature≤ 40 °C
Inlet pressure5–15 kPa, ±1 kPa
Pressure fluctuation≤ 10% per minute

The most common mistake: accepting raw POME gas at 2,000–4,000 ppm H₂S. It halves lube-oil life and doubles maintenance cost, invisibly, until the first overhaul.

Seven pieces of data we need

Before we quote

  • FFB throughput t/h and operating days
  • POME pond: stages, volume, HRT, covered or not
  • Measured biogas flow and composition
  • Electricity tariff or diesel consumption and price
  • Grid connection status and transformer capacity
  • Boiler fuel, steam demand and pressure
  • Load profile: base load, peaks, night shift
