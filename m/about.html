About KSTS

A Sabah contractor with the right registrations — and a factory behind it

KSTS K. S. Technical Services is a CIDB-registered, Bumiputera-owned technical services company based in Telupid, Sabah. We deliver biogas and bioenergy projects for the palm oil and agro-industrial sector in Sabah and Sarawak.

The company

What KSTS does

Founded to serve the estates and mills of the Labuk–Sugut and Telupid belt, KSTS works across civil, mechanical and electrical scope, and holds the statutory registrations a biogas plant needs before it can legally run.

  • Agricultural, building, civil and mechanical works
  • I.C.E. and steam engineering visitation under FMA 1967
  • Energy management, project management and technical solutions
  • Land transportation services
  • Drone aerial surveying and agricultural spraying

Managing Director

Sdr. Leong Kian Sheng

Known as Alvin · B.Eng.Tech(Mech Eng)(USQ)• Eng Tech (BEM)• ASEAN Eng Tech (AER)• IncIEM• MMOSTA• Grad Tech (MBOT)• TMIEAust

Alvin Leong has spent 22 years in the Sabah palm oil milling and plantation industry. He built his career at a palm oil mill in Sandakan (Kilang Kelapa Sawit Fortuna Sdn Bhd), rising from Assistant Mill Manager & Project Engineer to head of operations and development, and now serves as Managing Director of KSTS. His work covers palm-based biomass watertube steam boiler operations for CHP generation, green technology development projects including palm biomass composting for pelletised fertiliser, three field ESP installations for air pollution control, and feasibility studies for POME biogas power generation.

In the news

Invited to speak at the Institution of Engineers Malaysia, Sabah Branch

Alvin Leong was the speaker at the IEM Sabah Branch Continuing Professional Development talk, “Transforming Palm Oil Mill Biomass Wastes”, in Kota Kinabalu. The session covered cogeneration and Bio-CNG from palm kernel shells, palm fibre and POME, and the three constraints that decide whether a project actually gets built: securing a reliable biomass supply, the capital cost, and a long-term commercial arrangement.

Sabah has 129 active palm oil mills producing an estimated 25.75 million tonnes of biomass a year — the resource base behind every capacity we size.
