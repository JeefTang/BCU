Balance of plant

The parts between the pond and the engine

Capture

Pond cover & gas collection

HDPE or geomembrane cover, headers, condensate traps, relief and hatches.

Desulfurisation

Biological + iron oxide

Trickling filter for bulk H₂S, polishing vessels in duty/standby.

Conditioning

Chiller, blower, filters

Refrigerated dryer, coalescing filters, Roots blower with VFD, flame arrestor.

Storage

Gas holder

Membrane holder to buffer mill swings and protect the engine.

Safety

Flare & gas detection

Full-flow flare, CH₄/H₂S detection, ESD logic, Ex-rated instruments.

Heat

Heat recovery

Exhaust exchanger and jacket circuit to 85–95 °C hot water, or steam.
